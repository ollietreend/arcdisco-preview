Why choose A.R.C. Disco?

A small local business

We are a small local company (myself and my wife) based in Bishop’s Stortford, Hertfordshire. We have had many repeat bookings from clients and their guests.

Here are a few points to help you choose A.R.C. Disco for your function.

Remember, it’s your party we do!

At A.R.C. Disco we never forget this. Talking to our clients and finding out how they see their evening is the best way to get the party right for you. If you book an 80’s night, that’s what you will get. We won’t start playing current chart music halfway through (unless you change your mind)!

Feel free to make suggestions or ask questions via email, phone or post. We are happy to accommodate your requirements and have catered for many different occasions from a 13th birthday with a Hollywood red carpet theme to a 90th birthday bash.

Booking Us

We will not pass your booking or your details on to other companies without your permission. We are happy to meet you and discuss your plans.

You will be our only booking for the day of your function, once the date is confirmed.

Presentation is important to us. We will be dressed for the occasion, and our rig will be neat and tidy — no unsightly cables or mess.

When you make an enquiry, we will try to contact you via your preferred method within the next 24 hours. We will always use your preferred contact method.

Copies of our PAT (Portable Appliance Test) and PLI (Public Liability Insurance) certificates are available on request. Many venues require these certificates and can turn away suppliers who do not meet their requirements. View our public liability insurance.

We carry back-up equipment.

Equipment failure is rare with well-maintained professional kit. We carry a backup play-out system in our main console at all times, and our active speakers each have a built-in amplifier, so if one fails the other can finish the night. You probably won’t even notice. Only a power cut will stop us playing.
