Mobile Disco Services

Music & Lights

We will provide a sound system appropriate to your needs and run it at volumes that meet your requirements. With the use of intelligent lighting, your all-important first dance will not be ruined by madly flashing lights. You can request as many songs as you like before your event. We will fit them together to make a great night. With your permission we will happily take requests on the night. Your DJ will dress smartly as the occasion requires. If you require it, we can also arrange to meet with you before your event.

Bear in mind that most parties run for four to five hours. With an average song of three to four minutes, that allows roughly 75 songs — fewer once you account for announcements, speeches and requests from guests. It is worth choosing the songs you and your guests will want to dance to, rather than a list of all your favourites.

Mood lighting can transform your venue

Using modern LED lighting, we can change a room from its everyday form into something with real atmosphere. Vibrant colours of your choice complement your scheme and bring the room to life, giving it a sophisticated, elegant look.

We will deliver and install our lighting at your venue prior to the start of your function, ready for your enjoyment.

Giant numbers

Our Giant Illuminated Numbers make a great addition to your birthday celebrations. They are lit with RGB LEDs, can be any colour and can be matched to our uplighting package. They are four feet tall, finished in white, and can be made up for most milestone birthdays.

Please note that the balloon arch and silver curtain are not ours to hire.

LOVE letters

Like the giant numbers, our LOVE letters are lit with RGB LEDs, can be any colour and can be matched to our uplighting package. They are four feet tall and finished in white.

Early setup

Early setup is useful when timings are tight or access is limited, and can be combined with our background music service. It saves time and hassle later in the day. Please ask for details.

Background music

For your wedding breakfast, alongside an early setup, our background music service can keep guests entertained. We adapt the playlist to your guests, taking requests and including your own favourites. Background music is only available with an early setup and is not included automatically.
